## Planning and public data

### [AFDC tool library](https://afdc.energy.gov/tools)
Public resource

Browse calculators, maps and data searches for alternative fuels and energy-efficient vehicles.

Before you choose: Which assumptions and vehicle classes match your fleet?

### [Alternative Fueling Station Locator](https://afdc.energy.gov/stations)
Public resource

Find alternative fueling and charging locations.

Before you choose: Confirm connector type, access, availability and vehicle clearance before planning a trip.

### [Vehicle Cost Calculator](https://afdc.energy.gov/calc/)
Public calculator

Compare vehicle ownership costs using driving and fuel assumptions.

Before you choose: Use your duty cycle and local costs; check what the calculation includes.

### [AFLEET](https://afleet.esia.anl.gov/home/)
Argonne National Laboratory

Explore fleet petroleum use, ownership costs and emissions scenarios.

Before you choose: Document the selected model, assumptions and limitations before comparing scenarios.

### [EVI-X Toolbox](https://afdc.energy.gov/evi-x-toolbox)
Public modeling resources

Explore tools for electric-vehicle charging infrastructure scenarios.

Before you choose: Match the tool to your charging question and confirm the required input data.

## Fleet operations platforms

### [Fleetio](https://www.fleetio.com/)
Commercial platform

Fleet maintenance, inspections, work orders and parts inventory.

Before you choose: Check integrations with your current telematics, data export and support responsibilities.

### [Geotab](https://www.geotab.com/)
Commercial platform

Connected-vehicle telematics, fleet reporting and integration tools.

Before you choose: Check vehicle and device compatibility, data access, retention and integration costs.

### [Samsara](https://www.samsara.com/)
Commercial platform

Fleet telematics, GPS tracking, maintenance and connected operations.

Before you choose: Check hardware needs, implementation effort, permissions and contract terms.
